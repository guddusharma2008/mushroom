About

A family farm that grows, picks and pickles its own mushrooms.

is a family-run mushroom farm. We grow button, milky and oyster mushrooms ourselves, pick them by hand every morning and bring them straight to your kitchen.

We started by growing mushrooms for our own family and kept going because neighbours kept asking for more. Today we supply homes, cafés and restaurants nearby, and the way we work hasn't changed: grow clean, pick fresh, sell honestly.

Mushrooms that are perfectly good but not pretty enough for the pack go into our pickles. We make them in small batches with whole spices and cold-pressed oil, the way it's done at home.
